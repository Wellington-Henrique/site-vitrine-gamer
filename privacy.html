Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Stream Forge is a personal desktop application used by the owner of Vitrine Gamer to publish the channel's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It has no other users and collects no data from third parties.

What is used

  • From TikTok: the connected account's open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), and the access and refresh tokens needed to post (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • Nothing else. The app does not read followers, messages, comments, or any data of other users.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted by the operating system (Windows DPAPI), never in plain text and never in logs. No data is sent to any server other than the networks' own APIs, and nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Removal

Disconnecting the account in the app deletes its tokens and asks TikTok to revoke them. Access can also be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.

Changes

This policy may change; the date above shows the latest version.
